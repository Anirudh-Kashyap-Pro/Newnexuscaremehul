# 🌿 NexusCare Aura — Hackathon Healthcare Operating System

> **A polished, practical, trustworthy, and inclusive dark-themed healthcare web app designed to coordinate care sooner, eliminate avoidable hospital waiting, and empower patients, clinical staff, and hospital administrators.**

---

## 🌌 Dark Animated Background & Visual Identity
- **Dark Obsidian Forest Canvas** (`#040806`, `#060E0A`, `#0C1E15`): Immersive, calming, and visually commanding.
- **Ambient Glowing Aurora Orbs**: Floating biomorphic radial gradients (`auroraFloat1`, `auroraFloat2`, `auroraFloat3`) that drift smoothly in the background.
- **Bio-Grid Neural Overlay**: Subtle 48px mesh grid giving a high-tech clinical depth.
- **Luminescent Mint & Emerald Accents** (`#10B981`, `#34D399`, `#6EE7B7`): Bioluminescent status highlights and glowing borders.
- **Mirror Interface & Specular Reflections**: Glassmorphic cards with light-sweeping sheen highlights on hover.
- **Scroll Progress Glow Tracker**: A reactive emerald/mint beam fixed to the top of the viewport tracking reading depth.

## ♿ Inclusive Accessibility & Privacy
- **Text Scaler**: Accessible `A`, `A+`, `A++` text sizing across all views.
- **High Contrast Mode (WCAG AAA)**: Enhanced dark contrast with intense mint borders.
- **Reduced Motion**: Disables floating background orbs, 3D scanning beam, and marquee movement.
- **Multilingual Support**: English, Español, हिन्दी, Français, and 中文.
- **Caregiver Delegation & ABDM Audit Trail**: Immutable clinical access log.
